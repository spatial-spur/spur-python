Commuting-zone example data
===========================

Chetty, Hendren, Kline and Saez (2014), Where Is the Land of Opportunity?
The Geography of Intergenerational Mobility in the United States.
https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qju022
https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/NALG3E

The package converts the commuting-zone workbook from the replication
materials of Mueller and Watson (2024), Spatial Unit Roots and Spurious
Regression, to CSV.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11199509

The original data and replication deposits carry CC BY 4.0.
